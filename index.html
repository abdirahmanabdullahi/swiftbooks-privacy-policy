SwiftBooks Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SwiftBooks, a product of Ciyesystems ("we", "us"), collects, uses, discloses and protects information when you or your organisation ("you") use the Service. We act as a data processor for the personal information you upload about your customers and staff; you remain the data controller. We act as a data controller for account-level information about your organisation and its administrators.

Information we collect

We collect: (a) account information you provide, such as company name, contact details, billing information and the credentials of users you create; (b) operational data you enter into the Service, including ledger accounts, transactions, employee records, payroll data, purchasing and inventory records, and supporting documents; (c) technical data automatically generated when you use the Service, including IP addresses, device and browser identifiers, timestamps and pages or actions accessed; and (d) communications you send to us, such as support requests and feedback.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to: (a) provide, operate, maintain and improve the Service; (b) authenticate users and authorise access; (c) maintain the audit trail required for accounting and regulatory purposes; (d) detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and other harmful activity; (e) communicate with you about your account, billing, support and important changes to the Service; (f) comply with our legal obligations, including responding to lawful requests from public authorities; and (g) with your consent, send product updates and other communications, which you may opt out of at any time.

Lawful basis (for users in the EEA / UK)

Where applicable data protection law requires it, we rely on the following lawful bases: (a) performance of a contract — to provide the Service to your organisation; (b) legitimate interests — to operate and secure the Service, prevent fraud and improve our products; (c) compliance with a legal obligation — to satisfy regulatory, tax and accounting requirements; and (d) consent — for communications and any other use you have expressly agreed to.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with: (a) subprocessors who help operate the Service, such as cloud hosting, email delivery, error monitoring and analytics providers, under written contracts requiring appropriate safeguards; (b) professional advisors, including auditors and legal counsel, when reasonably necessary; (c) regulators, law enforcement or other public authorities when required by law or in response to valid legal process; and (d) a successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality.

International transfers

Personal information may be processed in countries other than the one in which it was collected. Where we transfer personal information across borders, we put in place appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection law, including standard contractual clauses where relevant.

Data security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These include encryption of data in transit, role-based access controls, audit logging, principle-of-least-privilege practices, secure software development, regular backups and vendor security reviews. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you without undue delay in line with applicable law.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and for as long thereafter as necessary to (a) provide the Service, (b) comply with our legal, accounting, tax and audit obligations, (c) resolve disputes, and (d) enforce our agreements. Operational data you create in the Service (transactions, ledger entries, audit trail) is retained for the periods required by applicable financial-services and tax regulations, which may extend several years beyond account termination.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to (a) access the personal information we hold about you, (b) request correction of inaccurate information, (c) request deletion in certain circumstances, (d) object to or restrict certain processing, (e) request portability of information you provided to us, and (f) withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@ciyesystems.online. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Where we act as a data processor on behalf of your organisation, requests from your organisation's customers should be directed to that organisation, and we will assist as required by our contract.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to authenticate users, maintain session state and protect against fraud. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how the Service is used and to improve it, where permitted by law and your settings. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling necessary cookies will prevent the Service from functioning.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to the email address associated with your account or shown in-app at least fourteen (14) days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was most recently revised.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to exercise your rights, can be sent to privacy@ciyesystems.online.
